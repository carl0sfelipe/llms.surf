The part worth reading: one billion llms-surf cloud tokens go to this lineup, and they do not drop flat. They drop by tier, and the tier is arithmetic you can read — not a promise you have to trust.

Haole ×1 — you opened the issue.
Grom ×5 — a signed run of your own, or a referral who actually contributed.
Local ×25 — several signed runs, or you reproduced someone else’s number.
The Legend ×100 — you caught a fake, or rebuilt other people’s numbers until they held.

Your share is one billion × your weight ÷ the sum of everyone’s weights. No fixed per-person number is promised, because a fixed number breaks the day too many people show up. The climb starts now: every contribution moves the arithmetic.

The dial lives in data/lineup-points.json and the queue in data/lineup.json, both in the repo. When the numbers change, a commit says so.

The tokens do not exist yet — the cloud is not live, nothing is credited today, and there is no date. The pledge and the arithmetic are what is fixed. Tier is computed from points alone; the machine does not claim to verify what it cannot see. Leaving costs one click. No emails after the drop unless you ask.
